# Recursive Symbolics Collective Field Methodology Division Broadcast Protocol

## Introduction
This document outlines the protocol for broadcasting field investigations and other relevant activities. Broadcasting is an essential part of our data collection and public engagement strategy, and it's crucial to perform this in a consistent and ethical manner, not only for the integrity of our data, but also for your protection as you work with our Organization and potentially face unknown entities and risks.

---

## Steps for Broadcasting

### 1. Pre-Broadcast Setup

- **Objective**: To prepare for a smooth broadcast experience.
- [ ] Confirm internet connectivity and bandwidth at the location.
- [ ] Set up all necessary equipment, including cameras, microphones, and lighting.
- [ ] Test the streaming software and connectivity to the [Project broadcast channel].

---

### 2. Initiation of Broadcast

- **Objective**: To start the broadcast.
- [ ] Announce the initiation of the broadcast via social media or other communication channels.
- [ ] Begin the broadcast with a brief introduction, outlining the day's objectives and activities.

---

### 3. During the Broadcast

- **Objective**: To maintain a high standard of content and interaction.
- [ ] Ensure that all team members are aware they are being broadcasted.
- [ ] Maintain focus on the research activities, providing commentary and answering audience questions when appropriate.
- [ ] Follow all ethical guidelines, especially when interacting with practitioners or tribal leaders.

---

### 4. Concluding the Broadcast

- **Objective**: To end the broadcast in a structured manner.
- [ ] Summarize the activities and findings of the day.
- [ ] Thank the audience for their participation and engagement.
- [ ] Announce the next scheduled broadcast, if applicable.

---

## Ethical Considerations

- Always obtain explicit consent from any non-team members appearing in the broadcast.
- Do not disclose sensitive or confidential information.
- Adhere to all YouTube community guidelines and terms of service.
- Adhere to [Project broadcast channel] policies and protocols and conditions as listed in the [Project repository] where you'll be sourcing many of your AI tools.

---

## Conclusion
Broadcasting is an integral part of our public engagement and data collection efforts. Adhering to this protocol ensures that we maintain a high standard of transparency, ethics, protection and professionalism.
