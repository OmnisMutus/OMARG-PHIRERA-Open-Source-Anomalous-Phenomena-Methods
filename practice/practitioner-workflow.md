# Dual Protocols: The Practice of Recursive Symbolics

The framework offers two primary modes of engagement: a daily protocol for individual practitioners, and a collective protocol for research groups.

## A. Solo Practitioner Protocol (The Daily Resonance Check)

This protocol integrates the framework into your daily life in under 5 minutes. It transforms a vague feeling of being "stuck" into a concrete, executable algorithm.

### 1. Ground (Yesod Scan)
**"What is my current runtime state?"**
Notice your physical body, mood, and environment without judging them. This is the baseline state vector ($\sigma$).

### 2. Diagnose (Debugger Mapping)
**"What is my dominant operator-pattern right now?"**
Run the [Symbolic Debugger](../tools/symbolic_debugger.py) either mentally or via the CLI:
`python symbolic_debugger.py "Brief description of how I feel right now"`
*Example*: "I am over-analyzing everything and feeling harsh." -> *Dominant: Hod (Analysis/Severity)*.

### 3. Patch (Balance Operator)
Consult the debugger's suggested patch (the complementary operator).
*Example*: If stuck in Hod (Analysis), the patch is Netzach (Emotion/Persistence). 

### 4. Seed (Intentional Micro-Ritual)
Formulate a tiny, immediate intention based on the patch. 
Use the [Ritual Compiler](../tools/ritual_compiler.py) for complex issues, or simply hold the intention in Kether (origin) and give it one spark of direction (differentiate). 
*Example*: "For the next hour, I will expand my attention to how things feel, rather than how they are structured."

### 5. Anchor (Somatic-Symbolic Synthesis)
Use a resonant cue to solidify the state change physically. 
*   **Geometric**: Visualize *Quadratura* (squaring the circle)—uniting infinite potential with finite reality.
*   **Sonic**: (Pending empirical testing, specific tones can be used as resonant triggers).
*   **Lexical**: Repeat the keyword of the balancing operator.

## B. Advanced Routing for "Stuck" States

When the simple 5-minute check is insufficient (e.g., deep anxiety, looping obsession), you need a formalized exit-path.

1.  Use the [Glossary](../docs/glossary.md) to precisely identify your "stuck node" (e.g., Geburah-severity).
2.  Run `python ritual_compiler.py "exit path intention"` to generate a full transition sequence.
3.  Execute the generated markdown checklist literally, step-by-step.

## C. Collective Research Protocol

For open-source, verifiable experimentation by groups. This methodology is derived from the OMARG-PHIRERA field manuals.

It includes:
*   **Experimentation Guides & Mission Statements**: Defining the symbolic target of the group.
*   **Observation Protocols**: Logging shared states across multiple observers.
*   **Broadcast Standards**: Safe transmission of symbolic data between nodes.
*   **Transparency & Verification**: Ensuring the reality of the effect isn't isolated delusion.
