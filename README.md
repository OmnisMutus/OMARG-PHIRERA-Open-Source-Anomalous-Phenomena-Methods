# Recursive Symbolics

### A Mathematical Philosophy of Consciousness Through Living Language

> **Every symbol is a process. Every process transforms consciousness. Every transformation is measurable.**

---

## Core Axiom

Reality is composed neither fundamentally of matter nor of mind, but of **recursive symbolic transformations**. Matter, mind, mathematics, and language are different manifestations of the same underlying recursive process.

---

## The Aim

Recursive Symbolics provides a unifying research program in which symbols, mathematics, computation, cognition, and contemplative practice are studied as aspects of recursive information processes. It generates testable models where possible and acknowledges when claims remain speculative.

> **The method of recursion. The language of mathematics. The realization of consciousness through symbolic transformation.**
