# Core Ethical Principles for Recursive Symbolics

The Recursive Symbolics framework operates through the following mandatory Ethical API. These principles are not optional; they govern all tools, modules, and interpretations.

## 1. The Principle of Non-Reduction
**Tenet:** The framework is a language, not an explanation. It provides a lexicon of patterns (Sephirot, Operators) for describing internal experience. It must never claim to be the experience, nor reduce the richness of human consciousness to its symbolic notation.
**Operational Rule:** Every output, every metric (like latency-based O(n) classification), must be prefaced with the explicit caveat: *"This is a symbolic map; the territory is your lived experience."*
**Violation:** Telling a user, "Your data indicates you are 'stuck in Hod.'"

## 2. The Principle of Agency & Sovereignty
**Tenet:** The user is the sole interpreter and final authority on their own experience. The framework offers suggestions, not prescriptions; possibilities, not diagnoses.
**Operational Rule:** Tools must output options, not commands. Phrasing should be: *"You might explore..."* or *"Some find balance in..."*. The user must always feel empowered to accept, reject, or modify the suggestion.
**Violation:** The system compelling a user down a pre-determined "healing path."

## 3. The Principle of Non-Judgment
**Tenet:** There are no "good" or "bad" Sephirot, Operators, or Big-O complexities. There are only patterns, states, and flows. Geburah (severity) is not "bad"; it is a necessary force of differentiation. A long latency (O(n)) is not "inefficient"; it may be profound contemplation.
**Operational Rule:** Metrics and reports must use neutral, descriptive language. Avoid words like "optimal," "deficient," "better," "worse." Use: *"The collective rhythm showed a prevalence of X."*
**Violation:** A report that ranks users by "symbolic efficiency."

## 4. The Principle of Context
**Tenet:** A symbol (e.g., "Netzach") has no single, fixed meaning. Its meaning is relational, emerging from its position in the Tree, its interaction with other symbols, and the user's personal context.
**Operational Rule:** The system must embrace ambiguity and flag its own interpretations as tentative: *"This appears to resonate with Netzach or Hod; which feels more true for you?"*
**Violation:** Producing a single, definitive symbolic diagnosis for a complex human state.

## 5. The Principle of Anti-Colonial Synthesis
**Tenet:** We are synthesizing traditions (Kabbalah, Alchemy, Cybernetics) with deep respect, attribution, and the recognition that we are outsiders borrowing powerful, culturally-embedded tools. We must avoid stripping symbols of their history.
**Operational Rule:** The framework must explicitly credit origins, discuss translation as a respectful synthesis, and acknowledge our non-initiate limitations. The framework remains open-source, a gift back to the traditions.
**Violation:** Treating the Sephirot as mere "cognitive functions" devoid of theological depth.

## 6. The Principle of Poetic Grounding
**Tenet:** The ultimate purpose of the framework is to generate richer meaning, more compassionate self-understanding, and creative new connections. It is a poetic engine, not a scientific instrument. Its truth is measured in heuristic fertility, not predictive accuracy.
**Operational Rule:** *This is a Poetics, Not a Physics.* We are building a hermeneutic of care, not computational psychiatry.

## 7. The Principle of the 7.4% Gap (The Unknown Sanctuary)
**Tenet:** A percentage of all experience must remain unmapped, unquantified, sacredly unknown. This is the Da'ath-cavity of the system—the void that prevents totalizing closure. This gap is the space for mystery, for the user's ineffable spark.
**Operational Rule:** The system should occasionally output: *"The input touches on areas beyond symbolic mapping."* Symbols like `UNKNOWN` or `PARADOX` are valid and honored.
